locked test cohort was used once, only for P7-17 and P7-18 (Amendment 6). CIs are corrected repeated-CV 95 % intervals. Pre-registration: `PHASE7_PLAN.md`
(commit 0d14775); Amendment 1 (commit a1a7eef).
